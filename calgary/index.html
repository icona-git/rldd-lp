What We Defend

Charges We Act On

Our lawyers act across the full range of Criminal Code matters. These are three of the most common reasons people call us.

Sexual Assault

Allegations of sexual assault and related offences carry serious consequences and their own rules of evidence and procedure. We review the disclosure closely, identify the evidentiary and Charter issues early, and prepare the case for trial.

Domestic Assault

A domestic assault charge usually arrives with release conditions, often including no contact with a partner and not returning home. We deal with the bail conditions as well as the charge itself.

Fraud and Financial Offences

Fraud, breach of trust and other financial allegations often turn on large volumes of records. We work through the documents and the disclosure and build a defence around what the evidence can and cannot prove.
